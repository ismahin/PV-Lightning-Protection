Mathematical conventions

Positive supercapacitor current charges from the DC bus into the capacitor. Vterminal = Vinternal + Isc*ESR and positive terminal power is absorbed energy. The bus equation subtracts positive charging current. SPD current is positive from the DC bus into the protective branch.
